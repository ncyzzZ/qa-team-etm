## Think like the user (3 lines)
- Who: the Turnaround Manager, a few times per event, when the event gets bigger or smaller, usually after weeks of editing the plan by hand and after job owners have already entered actuals.
- Wants: the new schedule shape without losing the actuals, the wording and the order they already fixed.
- Careless user: picks another size by mistake and clicks Confirm without reading; changes the size twice in a row; changes it, then leaves by the menu; switches to Minor and wonders why Save is dead; has a custom activity and does not know why the total is not 100.
