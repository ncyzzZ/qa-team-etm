# Draft test cases: ETM-5836 Manpower Mob Plan - Add & Delete Personnel (Personnel Roster)

Story: ETM-5836 (frozen exam copy, `story.md`). Source: story.md, `docs/UI_Map_ETM.md` index, `docs/modules/core-architecture.md`, `docs/modules/equipment-mob-plan.md` (only for what the product does on a similar editable table), `knowledge/`. No `analyst.md`, no `inspector.md`, no Figma for this run. Existing `[QA]` subtasks: none known.

Common precondition: logged in as Turnaround Manager (TM) who has the `Edit Manpower Mob Plan` permission; the Manpower Mob Plan dashboard of an Active plan is open; at least one Resource category row exists. At least one category comes from a snapshot of a Shutdown Logic resource and has Required Manpower dates. Role note: the team has only two roles, TM and Job Owner (JO); JO is view only. Sample names are made up (`Mali Test A`, `Preecha Test B`).

Notation: POS = valid flow, NEG = must be refused, BND = boundary. Priority comes from damage (the Story has none). The AC ids below are the numbering of the Story (no analyst file exists for this run): AC-1.1 inline button, AC-1.2 immediate row, AC-2.1.1 name entry, AC-2.1.2 name mandatory, AC-2.2.1 Accommodation, AC-2.2.2 D/N Shift, AC-2.2.3 Hot Bed, AC-2.3.1 free-text limits, AC-2.3.2 transportation dropdowns, AC-2.3.3 date pickers and autofill, AC-2.3.4 Onboard Date, AC-3.1 delete icon, AC-3.2 removal, AC-3.3 no confirmation.

## Think like the user (three lines)
- Who: a Turnaround Manager who rosters dozens of named workers per category in one sitting, often by pasting or tabbing quickly, and who corrects mistakes by deleting the row.
- Wants: add a row, fill it fast, delete a wrong row without a pop-up, press Save once.
- Careless / rushed: clicks `+ Add Personnel` twice, leaves a row half filled, picks a transport (autofill) after typing one date, deletes a row and then presses Refresh, pastes names with spaces, works in two tabs.
