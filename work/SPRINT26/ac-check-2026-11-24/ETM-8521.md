## Problems
1. **"Faster" is never measured.** The only speed TC, ETM-8738, is manual and says "There is no time limit ... does not fail this case (BA, DEC-049)". No script records a paste duration (`pasteSteady`, paste.helpers.ts:152-156, waits for networkidle but does not time it). So AC 1's actual subject (response time) has no pass/fail check. Either BA gives a target or the card should be signed off on AC 2 only, stated explicitly.
2. **Scripts mostly fail; status of the app unknown.** run-results.json (20 Nov): passed 8752, 8760, 8763; failed 15; 8764 skipped (fixme). Triage shows locator/visibility causes (e.g. "Cell manpower of the row should be on screen", `dragTo` timeout on `.b-fill-handle`, `Gantt task not found: undefined` in 8743) and assertion failures (8737 "Source row 1 still has its value", 8741 "Edited cell shows the pasted value, not abc", 8740 "Row 2 of the last three shows 2", 8759 "6 resources rejected", 8762 "Row 10 invalid Manpower keeps old value"). None is linked to a defect and none was triaged as app vs script. Several are possibly real regressions (8741, 8759, 8762) and need a manual check before any Xray result.
3. **Scripts weaker than the TC:**
   - ETM-8737 step 7 (spec:102-106): TC says the two skipped cells of the non-contiguous copy are blank; script checks only the first Description and one Duration. Step 6 Ctrl+click selector is `TODO verify` (spec:99).
   - ETM-8736 step 6 (spec:56-60): "column left of the focused cell unchanged" is not checked; "row above unchanged" is only `not.toBe('Pump overhaul A')`.
   - ETM-8739 step 5 (spec:130-134) and ETM-8741 (spec:174, 184): selectors marked `TODO verify selector`.
   - ETM-8753 step 7 (spec:416): Work Conflicted checks only `nameA`, not both names.
   - ETM-8759 step 3 (spec:450-455): TC checks POB/Man-hrs after *each* rejected line; script checks once after a fresh `ECM Fitter[0]`.
