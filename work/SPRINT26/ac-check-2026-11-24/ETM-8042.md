## Problems

**TC vs AC, BA answers or another Story**
1. **ETM-8840 step 7** expects "the end of the **yellow dashed** Planned S-Curve". ETM-8003 AC1 makes the Plan line blue `#1677FF`. Fix the wording: say "Plan (dashed) line", not the color.
2. **ETM-8851 steps 3, 4, 7, 8** expect an invalid paste to leave the part empty with Save disabled. The BA answer S1 (round 5, 18 Nov) says the field keeps its previous value. The TC and the script (MAIN:454) were changed to the app's behavior by a user decision on 20 Nov, not by the BA. Either confirm with the BA or log a defect.
3. **ETM-8849 step 5** expects the Planned S-Curve of an old report to end on `15 Jul 2026`. On QA it ends around 12 Apr 2027. BA question 9 is still open (not sent). The TC states an answer the BA has not given.
4. **ETM-8846 step 5** expects an Excel download for the 3-year Plan. QA shows `Excel File` disabled. BA question 10 is open (not sent).
5. **ETM-8838 step 3** (Shutdown Logic PDF Day 1) and **ETM-8840 steps 3-4** (Gantt range and scale) have only "write down" results. AC 8.1 (PDF Day-1 rule) and AC 8.2 (Gantt scale) cannot fail.
6. **ETM-8833 (AC 3.1)**: the exact adjusted value is Dev-pending (BA Q5). Most steps "note down". That is acceptable only until Dev answers.
7. **ETM-8844** dropped the AC 9 "Ramp Down anchor when the Shutdown Size changes". Record that decision, or ask the BA.
