## Problems
1. **Speed limit has no BA source; no baseline.** ETM-8629 step 6 and ETM-8630 step 8: "15 s or less (Pass), 15-20 s (Pass, noted), over 20 s (Fail)". draft-tc.md:8 says "Limits (user, confirmed)" and "there is NO old build to compare with". The Story says only "faster" with no number, and the sibling card ETM-8521 got BA DEC-049 "no time target". So these two TCs can fail the card on a limit BA never set, while they cannot show "faster". Get BA to confirm the 15/20 s limit (or the DEC-049 rule) in Jira.
2. **TCs contradict each other on `+ Work Header`.** ETM-8632 step 5: `+` of Work Header -> "Platform, Work Header and Work rows visible; count = P + H + W" (Work Breakdown still hidden). But ETM-8633 step 5 expects "row count equals F0" (all levels) and ETM-8635 step 7 expects "row count equals N0". The script flags this itself (`tc-vs-ac` notes at spec:408 and spec:548) and still asserts F0 / N0 (spec:409-410, 549-552). One side must change after asking BA / checking ETM-2431.
3. **Dependency lines (AC 2) are not verified, and ETM-8634 collapses the wrong level.**
   - Every line check is `note(... 'check-by-eye')` (spec:431, 445, 455, 466, 474, 484); `lines` is counted at step 1 but never compared (spec:444). TC steps 3, 5, 6, 8 ("does not point to empty space", "none missing or doubled") can never fail.
   - Finder (spec:435-442): condition `!a.isLeaf === false` picks a **leaf** task (Work Breakdown), then `header = a.parent.id`, which is the **Work** row, not the Work Header the TC asks to collapse (TC step 3 "Collapse the Work Header that holds one of the two Works"). TC also says "two linked Works", script uses Work Breakdown rows.
4. **False-pass risk in ETM-8630 / 8632 / 8633 / 8634 / 8635:** `level()` (spec:24-37) skips the click when the box is locked or shows the other sign, adds a note and returns; the step still passes. ETM-8630 only needs one measured time (spec:137 `toBeGreaterThan(0)`). Per team rule a skipped / not-proven step must not be reported as PASSED in Xray.
5. **Open questions with no written BA answer:** Q1 (which build/env has the change) and Q8 (meaning of the lighter Work Header box) in draft-tc.md:34-37. There is no `questions-for-BA.md` / `ba-answers.md` in `work/ETM-8449/`, so it is not visible whether BA was ever asked.
6. ETM-8631 steps 11-12 (spec:231-242): logout/login replaced by a second (Job Owner) browser context; recorded as `partial`. JO sees only Active plans (team note), so `PLAN-20K` must be an Active plan or the JO half fails.
7. Timing method: `timed()` (collapseExpand.helpers.ts) includes scrolling the row into view and a "two equal reads" stability poll (intervals up to 400 ms), so times are slightly high; fine for a 20 s limit, not for comparing builds.
